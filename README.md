## About Me

I am a graduate student at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU), focusing on AI/ML systems, hyperparameter optimization, federated learning, and LLM-based research tooling.

My current work explores how LLM agents can assist Bayesian Optimization and Federated Learning Hyperparameter Optimization under limited evaluation budgets.

## Featured Projects

### HyperOpt Viz Studio

Interactive dashboard for analyzing and replaying hyperparameter optimization experiments.

- Built for inspecting optimization behavior across tasks, methods, and random seeds
- Supports experiment browsing, metric visualization, and search process replay
- Tech stack: TypeScript, React, Vite, Flask
